# BPM Platform - User Guide & Walkthrough

## Introduction
Welcome to the Biomass & Power Management (BPM) Platform. This application is an industrial-grade, real-time web application designed for monitoring and controlling hybrid solar-bioenergy microgrids.

This guide will walk you through the core functionalities and pages of the platform.

---

## 1. Authentication & Roles
The platform is secured using Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). 

### Available Roles:
*   **Admin**: Full access to all features, including User Management, Tariff configurations, and Threshold settings.
*   **Operator**: Access to real-time monitoring, dashboard, and the ability to view/manage maintenance tickets.
*   **Viewer (General User)**: Read-only access to the dashboard and analytics.

### Login Instructions:
1. Navigate to the root URL (e.g., `http://localhost:5173`).
2. Use the provided demo credentials to log in. For quick access, you can click the quick-fill buttons for Admin, Operator, or Viewer on the login screen.
3. Click "Sign In" to proceed.

## 2. Platform Navigation (Sidebar)

Once logged in, the Sidebar on the left provides access to the following modules depending on your role:

*   **Home**: High-level KPIs and quick summaries.
*   **Dashboard**: Real-time telemetry, animated gauges, and live power flow diagrams.
*   **Analytics**: Historical data visualization, reporting, and ROI calculations.
*   **Storage & Pneumatics**: Deep dive into Battery State of Charge/Health and Pneumatic Vessel Pressure.
*   **Alerts & Tickets**: Kanban board for tracking system alerts and maintenance activities.
*   **Environmental (ESG)**: Sustainability KPIs and printable ESG reports.
*   **Admin Panel** *(Admin only)*: System configurations and audit logs.
*   **About / Help**: Glossaries, support directory, and documentation.

## 3. Core Modules Walkthrough

### 3.1. Home Page
The Home page gives you an instant snapshot of the microgrid's health. 
*   **Top KPIs**: Shows total energy generated, current load, and grid import/export balance.
*   **Recent Alerts**: Quick view of newly generated maintenance tickets.

### 3.2. Live Dashboard
This is the heart of the BPM platform.
*   **Animated Gauges**: Watch real-time metrics update every 2 seconds via WebSockets.
*   **Power Flow Diagram**: A visual representation of how energy is flowing between the Solar Array, Bioenergy Generator, Battery, and the Grid.

### 3.3. Analytics
Analyse historical performance and economic impact.
*   **Time Filters**: Toggle between 24h, 7d, and 30d views to analyze trends.
*   **ROI Calculator**: Real-time calculations of money saved by utilizing solar and bioenergy instead of importing from the grid during peak hours.

### 3.4. Storage & Pneumatics
Detailed insights into physical storage systems.
*   **Battery Degradation Curve**: Predicts the remaining lifespan of the battery system.
*   **Pneumatic Pressure**: Real-time gauge for tracking pneumatic vessel pressure against warning and critical thresholds.

### 3.5. Alerts & Maintenance (Kanban Board)
Manage system issues effectively.
*   **Kanban View**: Tickets are organized into "New", "In Progress", and "Resolved" columns.
*   **Telemetry Snapshot**: Clicking on any critical ticket opens a drawer that displays the exact system state (snapshot) at the time the alert was triggered.

### 3.6. Environmental (ESG)
Track sustainability goals and generate reports.
*   **Carbon Offset**: Real-time counter of CO2 emissions avoided.
*   **Export Report**: Click the "Export ESG Report (PDF)" button to generate a beautifully formatted, printer-friendly summary of the microgrid's environmental impact.

### 3.7. Admin Panel
System configuration area (restricted to Admins).
*   **Users Tab**: Manage user accounts and roles.
*   **Thresholds Tab**: Define the warning and critical boundaries for system metrics (e.g., Battery SoC, Pneumatic Pressure).
*   **Tariffs Tab**: Configure peak, off-peak, and shoulder utility rates.
*   **Audit Log**: Track all system changes and actions performed by users.
